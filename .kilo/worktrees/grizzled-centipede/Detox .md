---

# **1\. PRODUCT OVERVIEW**

## **Product Name**

# **AI 360**

## **Tagline**

> **Use AI. Understand AI. Don't Depend on AI.**

AI 360 is an AI-powered digital wellness platform designed primarily for students.

The application helps users:

* understand their digital habits  
* improve focus  
* reduce unnecessary technology use  
* understand their dependence on AI  
* use AI as a learning assistant rather than a replacement for thinking  
* develop healthier digital habits  
* interact with AI using text and voice  
* track their progress

The central concept is intentionally different from a normal AI chatbot.

AI 360 uses **AI to help people use technology more intelligently and intentionally.**

---

# **2\. EXHIBITION CONCEPT**

The exhibition theme is:

# **AI 360**

The project demonstrates AI from multiple perspectives:

### **🧠 AI Intelligence**

Using AI to learn, reason, explain, and assist.

### **🌿 Digital Wellness**

Using technology without allowing it to dominate everyday life.

### **🛡️ Responsible AI**

Understanding AI limitations, verifying information, and maintaining independent thinking.

### **🎙️ Human–AI Interaction**

Using natural voice and conversational interaction.

Therefore:

> **AI 360 is not an app that tells people to stop using AI. It teaches them how to use AI without losing their own ability to think.**

# **3\. PROBLEM STATEMENT**

Students increasingly use:

* smartphones  
* social media  
* online entertainment  
* AI chatbots  
* search engines  
* digital learning platforms

Technology provides enormous benefits, but excessive or unintentional use can reduce focus and encourage dependence.

AI introduces another challenge.

Students can use AI to:

* understand concepts  
* generate ideas  
* receive explanations

but can also become dependent on AI to:

* solve every problem  
* complete assignments  
* avoid thinking  
* make decisions

AI 360 addresses both problems.
